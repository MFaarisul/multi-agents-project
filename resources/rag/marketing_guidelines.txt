Marketing Spend Guidelines

Budget Allocation
- No single campaign may exceed 50% of the total quarterly budget.
- The 'Organic' channel is excluded from spend caps.
- ROI = (revenue attributed to campaign) / (campaign spent).

Reporting
- Campaigns are grouped by quarter (Q1-Q4) and campaign_year.
- A campaign is considered underperforming if spent exceeds 90% of budget
  while attributed revenue is below 2x the spend.
