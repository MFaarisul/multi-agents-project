Eligibility
- Refunds are available for orders with order_status = 'completed' requested
  within 30 days of the order created_at date.
- Subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing cycle has started, except
  in cases of billing error.
- The Enterprise plan tier is eligible for prorated refunds on cancellation.

Processing
- All refunds must be recorded in the refunds table with the original order_id.
- The amount must not exceed the original order amount.
- The associated order must be updated to order_status = 'refunded' once the
  refund is issued.

Reasons
- Acceptable reasons: 'Customer dissatisfaction', 'Accidental duplicate
  purchase', 'Billing error', 'Service downgrade'.
